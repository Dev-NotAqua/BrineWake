# Sea of Thieves reference notes (for Brinewake's own art)

Reference board: `Reference\SoT\` (community wiki images, study only, see its `README.txt`).
Sheets studied on 2026-10-11: outposts (Golden Sands, Sanctuary, Ancient Spire, Plunder, Dagger Tooth),
islands (Lonely Isle, Lookout Point, Cannon Cove, Discovery Ridge, Devil's Ridge, Mutineer Rock), main menu screens.

## Rocks
- Not faceted shards. Every rock is a **worn slab**: a box that was cut by a few planes, then given very wide soft bevels.
- Broad, almost flat faces; rounded edges that catch a pale highlight; no small noise.
- Cliffs are piles of these slabs, leaning on each other at 10 to 35 degrees, big ones at the bottom.
- Colour: cream / warm tan in sun, cool blue-grey in shade, darker and greener at the foot. One soft gradient per slab.
- Tops carry grass caps and bushes; vines hang as leafy curtains down the sunny faces.
- Beach rocks are the same slabs, half buried, often in threes.

## Terrain
- Islands are **low**. Height comes from rock piles, not from hills in the sand.
- Outline is lobed: sand spits, coves, small islets joined by sandbars.
- White sand; a wide shallow shelf around it shows as a pale turquoise halo before the sea turns deep teal.
- Grass sits in soft bright patches inland and on rock tops, with a darker rim. No noisy speckle.
- Dense clusters of palms and broad-leaf plants, bare sand between clusters.

## Outpost (not a village)
- Tavern: the biggest building, roof like an upturned hull, a sail canopy over the door, round sign.
- Shipwright: open workshop on stilts over the water, tiled roof, stairs, barrels, hanging hook.
- Gold Hoarders: dark green canvas tent on the beach, banner pole beside it, rug and chests.
- Merchant Alliance: stall on the dock under a striped awning, ledger board, crates.
- Order of Souls: crooked hut on a raised deck, purple cloth, stairs, hanging bottles.
- Weaponsmith, clothing, equipment: small plank shops with awnings and signs.
- Long crooked dock, sometimes L shaped, posts at different heights. Lanterns everywhere, warm light.

## Menus
- Background: dim tavern interior, blurred, dark vignette.
- Top bar: tabs (Pirate, Discover, Play, Progress, Guilds), serif face, active tab underlined; player name top right.
- Heading top left: "Choose Your Experience", "Select a Ship Type".
- Choices are tall **cards** with rough torn edges: coloured header band (teal, purple, amber) with title and one line,
  painted picture below. Selected card has a pale outline.
- Bottom left: key hints (Select, Back, Settings). Bottom right: one framed action button.
- Pirate tab: list on the left (Choose Pirate, Create New Pirate), line-up of pirates on the right.
- Title: logo on a plank sign over a painted scene.
