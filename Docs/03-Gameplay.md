# 03 — Gameplay

## On foot
Walk, sprint, jump, swim, dive (oxygen), ladders, carry loot (two-handed),
sit, sleep, emotes.
Tools: compass, map, watch, lantern, spyglass, shovel, bucket, trumpet,
fishing rod, tankard, 4 instruments (squeezebox, hurdy-gurdy, banjo, drum).
Weapons: carry 2 of cutlass/flintlock/scattergun/long rifle; 5 shots each;
ammo chest. Food: fruit instant; fish/meat raw→cooked→burnt.

## Ships
Stations: wheel, sail length/angle, capstan, cannons, map/voyage/banner tables,
armoury, ammo chest, stove, bell, lanterns, storage, crow's nest.
Damage: 3 hole sizes at fixed sockets, flooding, bailing, planks, sinking.
Later: fire, mast break. All server-auth, replicated.

## Voyages (table: up to 4 proposals, majority vote, solo instant)
- Ledger: X-marks + riddle maps → chests. Wardens: skeleton captain → skull.
- Tradewind: timed fragile cargo, animal capture. Anglers: fish/cook/sell.
Emergent: bottles, barrels, wrecks, skeleton ships, forts, shark, monster later.
Loot: 5 tiers/type. Trick: Weeping Coffer (leaks), Tipsy Chest (drunk).
Powder kegs. Banners: licence, grade 1-5, up to 2.5x, stealable.

## Economy / Death / Social
Gold; rep 1-50 + promotions; commendations 5 grades; titles. Legend door:
rank 50 in 3 factions.
Death: ghost ship 15-25s by crew size → respawn on ship. Sunk → new ship far;
loot floats ~5min. Mermaid returns stranded.
Modes: Open Seas (PvP full), Calm Seas (private, reduced). Crews 1-4,
open/closed, invites, proximity voice, chat, phrase wheel, brig vote.
Target M9: 5 ships / 16 players. M1: 1 ship / 4 players listen-server.
